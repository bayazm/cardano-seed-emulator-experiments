# Experimental evidence

The following entries derive from existing research evidence. The validation matrix records new checks separately. Paths in the evidence column identify files in the research archive unless stated otherwise. These evidence paths are provenance citations. They are private archive records and are not inputs to the release instructions. Raw PCAPs and transaction records stay with the research archive. For fresh B11 observations see the [controlled runtime result](../audit/b11-runtime-result.md).

| Experiment | Configuration and method | Observed result | Evidence | Interpretation and limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B11 | Private chain configured with three producers and a relay. Historical observer recorded CLI queries and block/slot observations. | Baseline block observation CSV contains 32 records. The adoption baseline has 319 records; the delay CSV has only a header. | `captures/cardano_block_observations.csv`, `captures/cardano_adoption_baseline.csv`, `captures/cardano_adoption_delay50.csv` | Archive filenames identify baseline and delay runs. No delay estimate is supported by the empty delay dataset. Exact genesis pairing with each baseline file remains unverified. |
| B12 | Historical alternating transfers with and without metadata, four queried nodes. Counted confirmation statuses and calculated per-group medians from the CSV. | 20/20 `CONFIRMED_ALL` in the ten-pair run. Baseline vs metadata medians were 292 vs 441 bytes, 392 vs 541 lovelace, and 5.6132 vs 18.0645 seconds to confirmation. A separate single pair was also marked confirmed on four nodes. | `B12-cardano/data/performance-test/results/benchmark_20261022T120846Z.csv`, `benchmark_20261022T120103Z.csv` | Confirmation was observed by UTXO polling in that deployment. Timing also reflects block leadership, query cadence and sample size. These results do not isolate a latency effect. |
| B13 interoperability v2 | A/B with distinct magic 5555/6666; inspected capture filenames for targeted diagnostics. | Cross-chain capture attempts are archived. | `captures/B13-interoperability-v2/B13_exp3_handshake_attempt2_AS172.pcap` | Capture provenance exists. Protocol handshake and ledger outcomes were not reconstructed for this release. |
| B13 same magic | Distinct genesis with matching magic and different historical starts. | Source configuration confirms the parameter contrast. | Historical `config/network-{A,B}/shelley-genesis.json` | Historical cross-chain behavior unverified. |
| B13 same time | Distinct genesis with matching magic and equal historical starts. | Source genesis hashes differ despite equal start times and magic. | Historical `config/network-{A,B}/shelley-genesis.json` | Historical cross-chain behavior unverified. |
| B13 node join | Staged same-ledger relay activation intended on A; B remains independent. | Source config links AS171 to A. | Historical `config/network-A/topology-node2.json` and launcher | Historical catch-up time and eventual convergence unverified. |
| B14 | Candidate AS154, capture filtered to TCP port 3001. Counted TCP flags in historical E2, E3 and E4 candidate captures. | E2 had two bare SYN and zero SYN-ACK packets. E3 had two SYN-ACK and 140 PSH-ACK packets. E4 had eight bare SYN and zero SYN-ACK packets, with other TCP traffic present. | `captures/*B14-E2-unauthorized-AS154*.pcap`, `*B14-E3-authorized-AS154*.pcap`, `*B14-E4-revocation-AS154*.pcap` | Traffic pattern is consistent with admission and later restriction of new connections during these windows. Aggregate flag counts include multiple flows, including E4 traffic and possible in-flight data. |
| B15 v2 | Candidate AS184 and ledger registry transition sequence; reviewed deployment artifact names and phase capture metadata. | Admission and revocation captures exist. The retained v1 project also records a separate baseline denial experiment. | `captures/B15-smart-authorization-v2/*E7*.pcap`, `*E11*.pcap`, `captures/B15-smart-authorization/B15-E1-AS183-iptables.txt` | End-to-end correspondence between these packet streams and confirmed registry states was not reconstructed. |
| B16 | N16 application-level identity tests; inspected structured gateway outcomes. | Impersonation rejected with `INVALID_SIGNATURE_OR_DID`. DID substitution rejected with `DID_NOT_ENROLLED_FOR_NODE`. Reuse of the same challenge and a response against a fresh challenge were rejected; fresh legitimate control authenticated. | `captures/B16-did-authentication/N16-identity-attacks/N16-{A,B,C}-*-result.json` | Probes cover the recorded endpoint and challenge lifecycle. Credential theft, restart persistence and concurrent or distributed replay protection remain outside the result. |
| B16 N17 | Revocation archive contains a post-submit registry query, controller evidence and paired firewall/capture records. | The controller recorded revision 2 and authorization false on AS180–183, then emitted `REVOKE_ENFORCED`. Its post-enforcement record shows AS184 ACCEPT absent and AS184/AS185 DROP present in INPUT and OUTPUT. | `captures/B16-did-authentication/N17-revocation/N17-registry-after-submit.json`, `N17-controller-enforcement.log` | These are controller observations from the historical run. The new helper and network phase were not rerun during publication preparation. |
